Installation, tutorials, and topic guides for OpenVCAD 3.x (attribute modeling).

- :doc:`introduction/index`: install OpenVCAD, learn why it models volumes, and write your first designs.
- :doc:`modeling/index`: a catalog of primitives, then offsets, blends, arrays, sweeps, deformations, and surface relief.
- :doc:`fields/index`: grade material and drive geometry with fields from expressions, Python, volumes, and simulations.
- :doc:`imports/index`: bring in CadQuery models, textured meshes, and 3MF beam lattices, and select mesh regions.
- :doc:`metamaterials/index`: unit cells, cell maps, graded and conformal lattices, and their characterization.
- :doc:`analysis/index`: measure mass, wall thickness, and pore size, and find inkjet support volumes.
- :doc:`compilers/index`: compile designs for printers and simulation, convert attributes, and export meshes.
