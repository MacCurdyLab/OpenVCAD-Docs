Analysis
========

Tools that measure a design: inkjet support volume (:class:`pyvcad.SupportAnalyzer`, see the
:doc:`/guides/analysis/support-analyzer` guide), mass properties (:func:`pyvcad.mass_properties`), and
wall thickness and pore size (:func:`pyvcad.local_thickness`, :func:`pyvcad.pore_size`), and
connected pieces of material (:func:`pyvcad.analyze_connected_components`).

Mass properties
---------------

:func:`mass_properties` integrates a design over a voxel grid with partial-volume weighting at
the surface. It returns volume, surface area, centroid, mass, centre of mass, the inertia tensor
and principal axes, per-material volumes, volume integrals of any scalar attribute, and an
estimate of the sampling error. Units are mm, g/cm^3, g and g mm^2.

Mass needs a density. It comes from a modelled ``density`` attribute, from volume fractions
times each material's density (the optional ``"density"`` key of a material configuration,
:meth:`MaterialDefs.density` and :meth:`MaterialDefs.set_density`), or from one uniform
``density`` for a design that is only geometry. :class:`MassSource` picks one when a design
offers several.

Wall thickness and pore size
----------------------------

:func:`local_thickness` gives, at every point of the material, the diameter of the largest ball
that fits there and contains the point; :func:`pore_size` does the same in the empty space. The
result is a volume-backed :class:`FloatAttribute` (set it on the analysed node to colour, grade or
drive geometry by thickness) plus a summary: the thinnest wall, a volume histogram and the
connected regions thinner than a threshold. Sharp convex edges read small in the field, as every
ball misses them, but only walls, struts, necks and gaps count as thin in the summary.

Connected components
--------------------

:func:`analyze_connected_components` samples a node on a voxel grid and counts the separate pieces
of solid material, for example to find floating islands in a lattice before printing.
:func:`label_connected_components` does the same for an occupancy array you already have.
