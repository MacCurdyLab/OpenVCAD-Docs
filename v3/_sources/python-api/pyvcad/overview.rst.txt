Bundled material definitions
----------------------------

``default_materials`` and ``j750_materials`` are :class:`MaterialDefs` instances loaded when
``pyvcad`` is imported. Pass them to compilers and renderers that need material colors and IDs.
The ``*_path`` strings point at the JSON files they were loaded from, for building your own
:class:`MaterialDefs` or copying a configuration as a starting point.
